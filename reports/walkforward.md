# Walk-forward backtest

One model per test year: for test year Y the training rows are every earlier bank-quarter whose outcome window closed before the first prediction date of Y (report 03-31 of Y plus the 60-day availability lag, spec rule 6.2), and the test rows are the label-complete reports dated in Y. Scores of all years are pooled into one ranking for the pooled rows. `texas` ranks by the Texas ratio without a fit; `logit` is the Prototype 1 regularised logit refitted on the v2 features; `gbdt` is the tuned gradient booster from `config/settings.yaml`; `hazard` is the one-quarter hazard converted with `1 - (1 - h)^H`. Metrics per year use the year's own ranking; `brier` is reported only for probability outputs.

## Against Prototype 1's regularised logit

- Fixed split (P1 `models/logit`, test 2010-2013, v1 features): PR-AUC 0.3867, recall@2% 0.7143, 833 failures among 118696 rows. Not comparable with the pooled rows: a different test period and one training window.
- Walk-forward pooled logit (same C, v2 features, one model per year): PR-AUC 0.3843, recall@2% 0.7713. This is the fair comparison for the other walk-forward rows.
- No other model beats the walk-forward logit on pooled PR-AUC.
